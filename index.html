The farm

Maroela is a family game farm in the Loskop Valley, owned and loved by the Basson family. Giraffe, eland, kudu, nyala and zebra graze between the koppies, and on a quiet evening you might even spot a leopard.

Say “I do” in the bushveld

A thatched chapel, a candlelit reception hall and the whole farm as your backdrop.

The chapel

Wooden pews, a thatched roof and open doors onto the veld. Ceremony, photos on the swing and drinks under the trees, all within a few steps.

The reception

Our hall seats around 150 guests, with catering from our own kitchen, a bar on site and décor available. Accommodation for the wedding party is right here too.

Functions & conferences

Around 40 delegates in the restaurant or 150 in the venue, with a screen and projector, coffee bar and breakfast, lunch or dinner. Birthdays and year-end functions welcome.
